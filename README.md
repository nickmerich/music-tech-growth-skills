# Music x Tech Growth Skills

Agent skills for growing music technology products: plugins, virtual instruments, AI music tools, DAW add-ons, music apps, and platforms for artists and producers. Covers the whole loop from understanding creators to getting them to a first sound, recruiting and activating creator partners, launching, pricing, measuring, and turning users into the people who sell it for you.

Built by Nick Merich ([GRYT](https://grytrunclub.com)) from growth work across music technology, creator partner programs, and music x endurance experiences.

**The idea in one line:** in music tech, the product wins when a creator makes something they love in the first session and shows someone else. Everything here is built around that moment.

## Ground rules

1. **First output beats features.** Measure time to first sound, not downloads.
2. **Clicks are not trials, trials are not sales, sales are not kept revenue.** Refunds and chargebacks come off. See [evidence rules](docs/evidence-rules.md).
3. **Demos are honest.** Show what the product does, on its own, with the settings disclosed.
4. **Every paid or gifted endorsement is disclosed.** ([FTC Endorsement Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking))
5. **Fewer, better partners.** Hand-pick and onboard hard instead of mass signups.
6. **Program changes are proposals.** Commission, windows, payouts, and pricing change only with owner approval and a list of every surface affected.
7. **Never promise earnings** to creators, and never imply trial signups earn commission unless the terms say so.
