# Pricing and Promotions

## Models

| Model | Good for | Watch |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Perpetual license | Plugins people keep for years | Revenue lumpy; plan paid upgrades |
| Subscription | Ongoing content, cloud, or AI compute | Churn; must keep delivering value |
| Rent-to-own | Lowering the entry price for expensive tools | Clear terms on ownership, pauses, cancellation |
| Bundle | Catalogs | Cannibalizing single sales |
| Free tier / lite | Top of funnel | Upgrade path must be obvious |
| Intro price | Launch | Real end date; don't extend it repeatedly |

## Promotions

- Pick a few sale moments a year and commit to them. Constant sales teach buyers to wait.
- Tie promotions to a reason: launch, update, anniversary, a creator's release.
- **Stack check before any promo:** list price − promo discount − affiliate commission − payment fees − refunds ≥ your margin floor. If not, change the promo or exclude affiliates by proposal.
- Price changes and sale terms are `PROPOSAL`s until the owner approves, with every surface listed (store, site, partner pages, emails, platform settings).

## Trials and refunds

Say exactly what a trial includes and how it ends. Report revenue after refunds. Trials are not sales.

## Tests

Test one thing at a time (price point, offer framing, bundle), with a sample size you can actually reach. If you can't reach it, make a reasoned decision and watch the trend instead of declaring a winner.
