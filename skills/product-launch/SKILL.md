# Product Launch

## Six weeks out

- **Positioning line:** "[Product] helps [segment] [do job] without [pain]." Test it on five real users.
- **Waitlist** with one question (DAW and OS) so launch messages are relevant.
- **Private beta** with 10 to 30 creators from `creator-recruiting`. Goal: bugs found, first outputs made, permission to use their results.

## Seeding (track every state)

Approved → license sent → activated → first use → posted. Give reviewers the first-output guide and honest talking points, not scripts. Ask for disclosure.

## Assets

Hero demo (before/after, settings disclosed), 3 to 5 short clips, a 10-minute walkthrough, preset or project downloads, screenshots, a one-page press sheet with platform requirements and price.

## Listings

Your site, plugin marketplaces and directories, relevant forums' product announcement areas (follow each forum's rules), and the DAW communities where it fits.

## Launch week

| Day | Do |
|-----|----|
| -1 | Waitlist "tomorrow" email |
| 0 | Launch email, posts, partner posts go live, listings live |
| 1 to 2 | Answer every comment and support ticket fast |
| 3 | Creator showcase: what beta users made (with permission) |
| 5 | Intro offer reminder if you have one, with a real end date |
| 7 | Week one recap internally: funnel numbers by stage |

## 30 days after

Fix the top install issues, ship a small update people asked for, reactivate quiet beta users, and move partners onto the activation ladder.

## Rules

Real end dates only. No fake scarcity. No "industry-standard" or "used by top producers" claims without proof and permission.
