# Creator Recruiting

## Sources, best first

1. Existing customers already posting about the product
2. Creators who reviewed or tutorialized a competitor
3. Niche creators (often under 50k followers) whose audience buys tools in your genre or use case
4. Educators, course makers, and community leaders
5. Big names, only after the program has proof

## Score fit (0 to 2 each, 10 max)

| Factor | 2 means |
|--------|---------|
| Audience match | Their viewers make the music your product is for |
| Buying signal | Comments ask "what plugin is that", past affiliate links get used |
| Content format | They already make demos, tutorials, or session breakdowns |
| Platform fit | Their OS and DAW work with the product |
| Relationship | Warm intro, customer, or prior contact |

Work 8 to 10 first. Under 5: skip.

## The send

Personal, short, specific to one of their videos or posts. One clear offer (free license to try, program terms link), and one question.

> Hey [Name], your [specific video] breakdown of [technique] is the clearest I've seen. We make [product], and I think it'd fit how you [workflow]. Want a license to try it, no strings? If you like it, we have a partner program with [one-line terms from the live page]. [Mac/Windows note]

30 to 40 thoughtful sends a day beats 300 templated ones.

## Tracker states

Sourced → Scored → Sent → Replied → License sent → Activated → Posted → Approved partner → First sale. Log the date and channel of each change. A send is not a reply; a license sent is not a post.

## Follow-up

One follow-up after about five days with something new (a preset, a feature they'd use). Then stop.

## Rules

Never promise earnings. Disclose the material connection in any content they make. Respect platform DM limits and anti-spam rules.
