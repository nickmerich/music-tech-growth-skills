# Community and Workshops

## Live workshops

- One job per session ("make a choir intro in 20 minutes").
- Everyone leaves with something saved.
- Record it; clip it; send the replay with the preset used.
- Invite new trial users first. Workshops are activation, not just marketing.

## Community space

Only start a Discord or forum if someone will be in it daily. Seed it with channels people need: help, share your work, feedback, feature requests. Respond to every first post.

## Challenges (beat, remix, preset)

- Official rules: eligibility, dates, how to enter, judging criteria, prizes and their value, rights (entrants keep ownership; you get a limited license to showcase), how winners are chosen and notified.
- Use skill-based judging. Random-draw giveaways, purchase requirements, and some regions have legal rules; check before launching.
- Stems and samples: license terms for entrants must be clear.
- Showcase with permission.

## Lifecycle

| When | Touch |
|------|-------|
| Day 2 | "Did it load? Here's the 10-minute first-output guide" |
| Day 7 | One technique they haven't tried |
| Day 14 | Invite to a workshop |
| Day 30 | Ask for a review if they've used it 3+ times |
| Update ships | What's new, one example |

## Measure

Second-session rate, workshop attendees who make a first output, reviews collected, upgrade rate.
