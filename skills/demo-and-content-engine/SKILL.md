# Demo and Content Engine

## Honest demo standard

- Before/after or dry/wet in the same clip.
- Say what else is processing the sound. If it's the product alone, say so.
- Show settings or offer the preset.
- No undisclosed post-processing, stacked chains passed off as one plugin, or "made entirely with" claims that aren't true.

## Formats that work

| Format | Length | Hook |
|--------|--------|------|
| Before/after | 10 to 30 sec | The "after" lands in the first 3 seconds |
| "Making a beat with only X" | 30 to 90 sec | Constraint |
| Fix a real problem | 30 to 60 sec | "If your vocals sound thin..." |
| DAW tutorial | 5 to 12 min | One job, start to finish |
| Session breakdown with a creator | 10 to 20 min | Their track, their process |
| Preset or project drop | Post + download | Something they can open and use today |

Sound-on platforms reward audio that changes fast. Caption the key line for people watching muted.

## Weekly cadence (one person)

- Monday: record 3 short clips in one session.
- Wednesday: one tutorial or creator breakdown.
- Friday: one preset or project share.
- Repurpose each to every platform you actually use. Skip platforms you can't sustain.

## Shareable product moments

Presets, projects, and templates that carry the creator's name and a tracked link turn users into distributors. Make sharing a button, not a chore.

## Measure

Saves, shares, link clicks, trial starts from content, and first outputs. Views alone don't tell you much.
