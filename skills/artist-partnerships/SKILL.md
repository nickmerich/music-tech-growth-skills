# Artist Partnerships

## Shapes

| Shape | What it is | Best when |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Signature content | Artist's presets, kits, or templates | Artist has a recognizable sound |
| Release tie-in | Product used on a release; behind-the-scenes content | Release is soon and real |
| Artist-in-residence | A season of content and feedback | Long-term fit |
| Creator collab | One video or session | Testing fit |
| Event | Workshop, listening session, IRL activation | Artist has a local or community pull |

Authenticity first: did they use the product (or the category) before you asked?

## Money

Flat fee, revenue share on signature products, affiliate terms, product, or a mix. Write down which money pays for what.

## Rights checklist (written before anything is public)

- Name, likeness, logos: approved uses and approval windows
- **Organic vs paid usage:** an artist posting is not permission for you to run it as an ad. Paid usage needs explicit terms, scope, duration, and usually extra payment.
- Content ownership and reuse window
- Music in content: sync rights for composition and master; samples cleared
- Signature product: who owns the presets or samples, royalty terms, what happens if the deal ends
- Exclusivities and conflicts on both sides
- Disclosure in every post ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking))

## Process

Discovery call → written terms (AGREED, PROPOSED, OPEN per line) → content plan → approvals → publish → report results back to the artist with real numbers.
