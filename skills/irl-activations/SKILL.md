# IRL Activations

## Pick one goal

Partner recruiting, first outputs for new users, press and creator relationships, or community for existing users. One goal decides the format.

## Formats

| Format | Goal it serves |
|--------|----------------|
| Hands-on demo station with headphones | First outputs |
| Producer meetup or beat session | Community, recruiting |
| Listening session with an artist | Relationships, content |
| Conference morning run or walk | Relationships in a relaxed setting |
| Workshop in a store or studio | First outputs, sales |

Music x endurance (a run, walk, or ride with an artist and producers) works when it's authentic to the people involved. For production, permits, and safety, use the Artist-Led Athletic Events skills.

## On site

- Get each person to make or hear something specific to them.
- Capture contact only with consent, and say exactly what you'll send.
- One follow-up offer tied to what they did on site.

## Follow-up (within 48 hours)

Personal note, the preset or session file from the day, and one next step. Track: contacts with consent → follow-up sent → trial → first output → paid.

## Reporting

Cost, verified attendance, consented contacts, and downstream conversions you can actually trace. Mark untraceable influence as such instead of inflating it.

## Negotiating with organizers

Offer something useful to their attendees. Get any comp tickets, revenue share, or promotion terms in writing; a proposal you sent isn't an agreement.
